# 2026-10-06 — The Remy-Sport site, built with the harness; emdashcms.com as the reference

**Status:** active — **3 of 13 done** — item 2 still has open decisions that are the owner's; items 5 to 10 wait on them

Two linked things. First: keep a reference checkout of EmDash's own site,
`emdash-cms/emdashcms.com`, the way we keep the templates and the EmDash source — because it is the
one production EmDash site whose source we can read. That is item 1, and it is done. Second: put a
real site up, on EmDash, using only this harness.

The owner has said which site: **it is for the Remy-Sport project**, and its repo is a new one,
`joeblew999/remy-sport-emdash`. Remy-Sport is a basketball events platform for Thailand. Its app is
live and cannot be indexed; its blog has one post and no home. What the project is, what exists and
what its site needs — with sources — is in [`../remy-sport.md`](../remy-sport.md). Read it first.

This is still the real test of the harness. Every flow here has been proven on a template with a
seed on top. Nobody has yet built a site with it. Where the harness cannot do what this site needs,
that is a harness item, written below as its own checkbox marked **gap** — the site drives the fixes.

What the reference site teaches is in [`../emdashcms-com.md`](../emdashcms-com.md). EmDash itself
is in [`../emdash.md`](../emdash.md).

**Effort, honestly.** Item 2 is the owner's: minutes each, once decided, but two of them (the name
and the domain) wait on a brand question the founders have left open since May. Item 3 is an hour
if nothing breaks. Item 4 is a day and is the one everything else stands on. Items 5 and 6 are a
day together for the structure. Item 7 is half a day for the list and two days for the block plugin,
which is not needed first. Item 8 is two hours plus a harness gap. Items 9 to 11 are two days, most
of it waiting on Cloudflare and on other plans. Items 12 and 13 are a day and do not depend on the
site.

**Not in this plan, and not small:** the design, the words, and the Thai. emdashcms.com is about
11,600 lines of `src/`. The Thai in the app and the help site is machine-translated and unreviewed
(`remy-sport/docs/README.md`, Translation provenance); a public site in Thai needs a person who
reads Thai. Only the Product Owner can write the positioning.
